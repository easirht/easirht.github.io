Academic Project

Three-Axis Robotic Arm

Designed and built a robotic arm with three-axis rotation using servo motors and an Arduino Uno, including full circuit design and button-based control logic.

Arduino Uno Servo Motors Circuit Design
View Project Details →

The arm uses three servo motors, each independently controlled through a dedicated push-button pair wired to the Arduino Uno's digital pins — one button to rotate a joint forward, the other in reverse — rather than a single continuous-motion controller. The circuit was built and tested on a breadboard before being wired into the final frame, with each servo's power draw and signal line kept separate to avoid voltage drops affecting motion accuracy. The build served as a hands-on introduction to embedded control logic — sequencing multiple actuators from simple digital inputs — which is the same basic principle scaled up in most automation and robotics systems.

Team Project — Formula Kart Design Challenge

Go-Kart Quality Control — Team Annexe RUET

Led quality control for the design and manufacturing of a Go-Kart competing in the FKDC — materials selection, dimensional analysis, and component sourcing, from workshop fabrication through to the final test run.

Quality Control Dimensional Analysis ANSYS FKDC
View Project Details →

As QC Team Lead, I was responsible for the chassis and drivetrain components standing up to real competition conditions rather than just passing a bench test. That meant checking incoming materials and dimensions against the design tolerances before parts went into the frame, tracking down fit issues during welding and assembly (visible in the late-night build sessions), and sourcing components on a tight budget without compromising the safety-critical parts — steering, brakes, and the roll structure. The kart competed at the Formula Kart Design Challenge (FKDC) in Coimbatore, India, representing Team Annexe RUET, and the team used ANSYS for structural checks on the chassis design before fabrication began.
