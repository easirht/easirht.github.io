Research

Where mechanical engineering meets machine learning, and the publications that have come out of it so far.

Research Focus

My research interests lie at the intersection of mechanical engineering and artificial intelligence — applying machine learning, deep learning, and image processing to problems in automation, thermal systems, and intelligent mechanical design. I'm drawn to work where an intelligent method has to eventually operate inside a physical constraint: power, weight, latency, safety, or manufacturability.

Publications

Development of an ANN-Based Indoor Thermal Comfort Prediction and Monitoring System

Submitted — ICCIT 2026, Cox's Bazar

Md. Easir Rahat, Md Ferdous Al Farabe

An ensemble artificial neural network predicting indoor thermal comfort in real time, benchmarked against Random Forest, XGBoost, and Linear Regression, validated against the CBE Thermal Comfort Tool, and deployed on embedded (ESP32) hardware with a live monitoring dashboard.

Neural Networks Thermal Comfort Embedded Systems

Revisiting Design Choices in LoRA-Based Continual Learning for Vision Transformers in Semantic Segmentation: An Empirical Study

Submitted — ICCIT 2026, Cox's Bazar

Md. Easir Rahat, Tajbir Hasan Shuvo

An empirical study reimplementing a LoRA-based continual learning pipeline (CLoRA) on a frozen ViT-B/16 backbone, systematically varying backbone pretraining, decoder depth, distillation weight, and LoRA rank across four PASCAL VOC splits to isolate which design choices actually drive performance and forgetting resistance.

Continual Learning LoRA Vision Transformers Semantic Segmentation
